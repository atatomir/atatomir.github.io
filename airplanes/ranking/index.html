Which fleets are hardest for the Impossible AI?

We played the Impossible AI against every possible fleet: all ways to place 3 planes on the board without overlapping. Each layout was played with different random tie-breaks, and the ranking uses the average number of shots the AI needed to down all three planes.

Opening shots hit tails

The AI opens in the middle of the board. Against edge planes those shots only find tail ends, which say little about which way a plane faces.

Noses come last

Few plane positions put a nose on an edge cell, so its answer looks predictable and scores low entropy. The AI shoots those cells at the very end.

How this was measured

The Impossible AI keeps every fleet layout that still fits the answers so far and fires where the answer is least predictable. Each shot is scored over six outcomes: miss, hit, or a nose facing up, right, down or left. A nose hit downs the plane and shows which way it faces.

All layouts were played to the end with the game's own AI code, once per random tie-break seed. Mirrored and rotated copies of a layout are grouped together ( groups), and each group shows its single hardest copy. The replay is one real game against that copy (seed 1), so its shot count can differ from the average.
