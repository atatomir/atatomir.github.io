How to play

Each player hides 3 planes on a 10×10 grid. Players take turns firing one shot. The answer is miss, hit (body, wings or tail), or nose: a nose hit brings the whole plane down. The first to down all 3 enemy planes wins. Online games check at the end that nobody lied about their answers.
