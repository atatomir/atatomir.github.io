About us

Founded in 2019, we are an information technology company developing screen recording and video editing software for home and office.

Our developers have 20+ years of experience in this area and want to build the complete toolbox for amateurs and professional editors alike. That is the reason behind our main product – ScreenToVideo.

After an incredible growth year, our team renamed the company to Tatomir N. Alex PFA in 2025. If you want to be part of our adventure, please contact us.
