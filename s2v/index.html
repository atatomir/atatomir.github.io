FAQ

Questions, answered

Can’t find what you’re looking for? Contact us — we reply within 24 hours.

Is there a free trial?

Yes. You can download a fully featured 15-day free trial. All of ScreenToVideo’s features are available, but your exported videos are watermarked. The projects you make with the trial are fully usable once you register.

Is it a subscription?

No. ScreenToVideo is a one-time purchase with no monthly or yearly fee. The license is perpetual and never expires, and it includes free lifetime upgrades and support.

Can I install it on more than one computer?

Yes. ScreenToVideo is licensed per user, not per machine, so the registered user can install it on as many computers as they own. Only the registered user may use the software.

Which formats can I export to?

MP4, WMV, animated GIF and animated WebP, plus audio-only MP3, WAV and M4A. You can also embed your video in an interactive HTML player or upload it directly to Google Drive and get a shareable link.

What do I need to run it?

A 64-bit version of Microsoft Windows 11, 10, 8 or 7, an Intel Core i-series or AMD Phenom II processor, 2 GB of RAM and 500 MB of disk space.

Are there discounts for schools or teams?

Yes. Students and teachers can get the Educational license for $79. Buying several licenses saves you 20% (2–5 licenses), 30% (6–10), 34% (11–20) or 36% (21+).

How do I receive my license key?

Orders are handled securely by PayPro Global. As soon as your order is processed, you receive an e-mail with your registration key, which activates the full version.

What if it’s not right for me?

Every purchase comes with a 30-day money-back guarantee.
